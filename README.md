Inferno Protocol is a co-op survival horror first-person shooter developed and published by Tektonic Interactive. It entered Early Access on August 28, 2026, for PC. The game supports solo play and online co-op for up to six players.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Inferno Protocol**. This trainer provides quality-of-life options for surviving Inferno Protocol's shifting labyrinth. It supports health protection, hunger and thirst management, resource control, faster crafting, ammunition assistance, and loader-based ability management.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore Inferno Protocol's labyrinth, test crafting and combat systems, experiment with loader abilities, or reduce survival-management demands during solo and private co-op sessions.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Inferno Protocol uses first-person movement across a large, circular labyrinth. loader abilities can provide movement advantages such as dash and double jump. Use movement options carefully in vertical areas, around ladders, and near environmental hazards.

### Tools & Inventory

Scavenge scrap metal, wires, ammunition, and other supplies. Creature remains can be harvested for materials including bones, armor, flesh, and organs. These resources are used to craft weapons, gear, traps, and other survival equipment. loaders should be reserved for situations where their abilities provide the greatest benefit.

### World & Environment

The maze changes as you progress and contains multiple layers and biomes, including Junkyard, Jungle, and Swamp. A central settlement provides farming, construction, crafting, and trading, while smaller camps are scattered through the corridors. Mark locations with spray, chalk, or notebooks to improve navigation and team coordination.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is presented from an FPS perspective and combines firearms, experimental weapons, traps, and environmental tactics. Creatures can react to sound and scent, and enemies may hear proximity chat. Harvest defeated creatures for crafting materials, place traps in narrow corridors, and use loader abilities to adapt to dangerous encounters.
